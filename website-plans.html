DIY builder or done-for-you?

A DIY platform such as Squarespace can be a sensible fit if you enjoy building and editing the site yourself. Little Technical Solutions is for owners who would rather hand the work to one direct point of contact.

No mystery about control

What you keep and how changes happen.

Your business assets stay yours

On every plan, you keep your domain, content, logo, photographs, and business data. Customer domains are registered in the customer's name.

Send changes the way you prefer

Use your LTS account, call, text, or email. Standard includes one small content change per month, Premium includes three, and Executive includes unlimited reasonable changes plus admin editing tools.

The ownership trade-off is clear

LTS owns and hosts a subscription build while the plan is active. You can buy it outright later, with subscription payments credited toward the buyout. If the plan ends without a buyout, the site comes offline only after written notice.

Featured Project

Armour Wireless Solutions, LLC

A custom credibility website for a specialized wireless consulting company serving carrier, partner, and government-facing audiences.

Industry

Wireless consulting and infrastructure support

Project Type

Responsive business website

Expanded Scope

Secure photo-management tools

Armour Wireless needed more than a digital brochure. The finished site had to explain the business clearly, introduce its owner separately, present restored archival photography consistently, and let the client manage project photos without editing code.

1. Brand and logo refresh

The existing identity was redrawn into a clean, consistent crest and carried through a professional Navy-inspired visual system.

2. Company and owner storytelling

The original About content became two focused pages: one for the company and one introducing Bill Armour, his experience, and leadership.

3. Photo restoration and presentation

Client photographs were digitally remastered, prepared for the web, and placed in uniform, responsive frames with polished captions.

4. Responsive custom design

Every page was built for desktop, tablet, and mobile around the refreshed Armour brand rather than a generic template.

5. Requested content and privacy updates

Contracting information, location language, calls to action, and other client-requested content were revised for clarity and privacy.

6. Secure client-managed gallery

Two-factor administrator access, image upload and removal tools, storage integration, and a public gallery let Bill manage photos without touching code.

Delivered outcome: One polished, mobile-ready destination for the company story, owner profile, capabilities, contracting information, contact options, and client-managed project photography.

“Dylan worked directly with me, communicated throughout the project, and built around what my company actually needed.”

Bill Armour, President / Owner, Armour Wireless Solutions, LLC

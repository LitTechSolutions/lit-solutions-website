The catch, stated plainly

On a subscription plan we own the website build and license it to you while you're subscribed. That's the trade for the low upfront cost. Plans run for a 12-month minimum, then month to month. If the subscription ends, we stop hosting the site and it goes offline — always after written notice. It is not rent-to-own.

Yours no matter what: your domain, your content, and your business data. If we registered your domain it's in your name from day one, and you get at least 30 days' notice before we stop covering the renewal. You can buy the site outright whenever you like, with everything you've already paid coming off the price.

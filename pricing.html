Pricing

Straightforward pricing. No surprises.

Published starting prices for common jobs, so you know roughly what you're in for before you call. Your exact quote depends on the specifics of the job, and we confirm it with you in writing before any work begins.

Introductory pricing: these are our introductory rates while we build up our client list. They aren't a countdown or a limited-time trick — when they change, we'll post the new rates here first, and any quote we've already given you is honored at the price we quoted.

How payment works

  • Website projects are 50/50. Half to get started, half at launch once you've seen the finished site and approved it. You're never asked to pay the whole amount before you've seen anything.
  • Repairs, installs and setups are billed when the work is done — not before.
  • The first diagnostic is free. We tell you what's wrong and what it costs to fix, and if you'd rather not go ahead, you owe nothing.
  • Our labor is warrantied for 30 days. If the thing we fixed breaks again in that window, we come back and re-do it at no labor charge.

Full detail in our Terms & Conditions — sections 3 (payment), 4 (cancellations and refunds) and 6A (workmanship warranty).

The catch, stated plainly

On a subscription plan, we own the website and license it to you while you're subscribed. That's the trade for the low upfront cost. If you cancel, or the subscription lapses, we stop hosting the site and it goes offline — we'll always warn you in writing first, but it does go dark. It is not rent-to-own, and it does not turn into ownership however long you pay.

What you keep no matter what: your domain, your content — text, photos, logo, anything you gave us or added yourself — and your business data, including every enquiry the site collected. Those are yours on any plan, in any circumstance, at no charge. If we registered your domain, it's in your name from day one and you get at least 30 days' written notice and transfer instructions before we stop covering the renewal.

You can buy it outright whenever you like. Tell us and we'll quote the buy-out — what the same build would have cost as a one-off project, less what you've already paid us on the plan, so none of your subscription money is wasted. Once it's paid, the site is yours under the same terms as the packages above, and you can drop to our regular $39/month care plan or host it yourself.

If you'd rather simply own your site from day one, take Starter or Business above — that's still the option we'd point most people to, and over several years it works out cheaper. The subscription exists for businesses who need to be online now and can't put several hundred dollars down today. Full detail in our Terms, section 9.

Have a proper look before you decide. Each plan has its own page with the full feature list, wireframes of what you actually get, exactly how the build starts once you sign up, and the ownership trade-off in plain English. Still unsure? Ask us — we would rather talk it through than have you pick wrong.

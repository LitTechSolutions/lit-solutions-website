How this actually starts

No surprises about what happens after you pay. This is the whole process.

  1. 1. Create your account

    Email and password, then a 6-digit code we email you straight away. It takes about a minute, and it's what gives you a dashboard, an inbox and a place for your paperwork.

  2. 2. Pay the $149 deposit from your dashboard

    One checkout, through Stripe — card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. The deposit isn't an extra fee, it's what reserves your build slot, and your $79/month plan starts in the same transaction.

  3. 3. Fill in your project brief

    It lands in your dashboard inbox the moment your payment is in — what your business does, who it's for, what you already have. A copy is saved to your Documents tab, and it's what we build from.

  4. 4. We call you within one business day

    A real conversation about your business, what the site needs to do, and what you already have — logo, photographs, existing copy. If you have none of that, we'll tell you what we need and how to get it.

  5. 5. You get a written scope of work

    Exactly what's being built, the pages, the features, and the timeline. It also states in writing that this is a subscription plan and what that means for ownership. Nothing is built until you've approved it.

  6. 6. We build it, and you review it

    Typically 1–2 weeks once we have your content. You see it on a private link before anyone else does, and you get revisions before launch.

  7. 7. It goes live, and we keep it running

    We handle the domain, hosting, SSL, and everything on the list above from that point on. You call us when you want something changed.

Before you pay, the trade-off in plain English

On a subscription plan we own the website build and license it to you for as long as you're subscribed. That is the trade for paying $149 instead of $699 upfront. It is not rent-to-own and it does not become yours over time.

If the subscription ends, we stop hosting the site and it goes offline — always after written notice, never as a surprise.

Yours regardless, in every circumstance, free: your domain, your content (text, photographs, logo, anything you gave us or added), and your business data including every enquiry the site collected. If we registered your domain it is in your name from day one, and you get at least 30 days' written notice before we stop covering the renewal.

You can buy it outright whenever you like — we quote what the build would have cost as a one-off, less everything you've already paid us. Nothing you've paid is wasted.

Full terms: Terms & Conditions, section 9. If you'd rather own it from day one, our one-off packages start at $699 and over a few years cost less.

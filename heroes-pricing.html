Who qualifies

Tap a category to see a quick thank-you and exactly what we need to verify your status.

We will never ask you to email us your SSN.
A DD-214 carries your Social Security number, date of birth and home of record. An LES carries your SSN and your pay and banking details. We're a cybersecurity company — we are not going to ask you to send either one as an ordinary email attachment, and you should be wary of any business that does.

So here's how we actually verify, whichever you prefer:
• In person — show us the credential at your appointment. Nothing is copied, scanned or kept.
• Redacted copy — send a copy with the SSN, date of birth and any financial figures blacked out. We only need to see your name and your status.
• License number — for doctors and other licensed professionals, give us the license number and we'll verify it directly with the issuing body.

Federal ID cards (military ID/CAC, veteran ID card, TSA credential) are verified in person only — consistent with 18 U.S.C. § 701, we can't accept a photo, scan, or copy of these.

Anything you do send us is deleted within 7 days of your eligibility being confirmed. We keep only the fact that you qualified and the date — so you verify once, and get the discount on every job after that, without us sitting on your paperwork. See our Privacy Policy.

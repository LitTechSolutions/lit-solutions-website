How a website gets built

From package to launch.

  1. 1

    Pick a starting package

    Starter or Business — flat-rate pricing, no surprises. See Pricing.

  2. 2

    Choose your exact features

    Use the Website Designer tool to pick what you want and watch your starting price update in real time.

  3. 3

    We build, you review

    We'll work with you directly — as hands-on or hands-off as you'd like — through one or two revision rounds before launch.

  4. 4

    Launch, with support included

    Every website launches with post-launch support built in, plus the option to keep it maintained long-term.

After launch

Support doesn't stop at delivery.

Starter websites include 30 days of post-launch support; Business websites include 60 days. Want help beyond that? The Website Care Plan covers hosting coordination, small content updates, platform health checks, and general maintenance — so your site stays online and current without you having to manage it.

What's next

Proprietary tools, built for our clients.

We're actively building software to make owning a website easier — the Website Designer tool is the first of it. Website Care Plan subscribers get access to new tools like it as they're released, at no additional cost.

Beyond websites, we also handle computer repair, networking, cybersecurity guidance, and general small business IT support — see the full lineup on Services.
